About

Pablo Motta

Software Engineer

Long story short, I was born in Brazil, moved to the USA in 2001, spent 10 years in Massachusetts, and have called Southern California home since 2010.

I’m a full-stack software engineer who enjoys building thoughtful, scalable web applications and the systems that support them. I’ve worked across customer-facing products, internal tools, CMS-driven platforms, performance initiatives, and modern frontend architecture.

What keeps me excited about software is how quickly the field evolves. I enjoy learning new technologies, understanding complex codebases, and using AI-assisted workflows to move faster, think through problems, and contribute more effectively.

My goal is to work with people who care about building useful, reliable software that creates real value for businesses and the people using it.

When I’m not coding, you’ll probably find me playing drums, watching soccer, staying active, or enjoying life with friends and family.
