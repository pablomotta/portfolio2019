I’m a full-stack engineer who’s passionate about using technology to solve real problems and build products that create genuine value.

A bit about me: I was born in Brazil, raised in the U.S., and I’ve called Southern California home for years now.

Over the years I’ve worked across customer-facing storefronts, internal tools, and CMS-driven platforms. I’ve helped lead a Shopify Hydrogen migration and performance work, built CMS-driven systems that let teams move faster, and supported and mentored the engineers around me along the way.

What I enjoy most is using technology to make things better — solving problems, reducing friction, and building solutions that provide lasting value. I like to keep learning as the field evolves and reach for whatever tools help me do that well.

More than anything, I’m passionate about collaborating with teams to build useful, impactful software that creates real value — for businesses and for the people who use it.

Outside of work, you’ll probably find me playing drums, watching soccer, staying active, and enjoying life with friends and family.
